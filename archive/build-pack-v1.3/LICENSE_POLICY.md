# License Policy

No public Narrivy source license is chosen by this build pack. During private development, do not add an invented SPDX identifier or copy the license of a reference project. Before public distribution, the owner must choose Narrivy's source/commercial licensing strategy. Third-party components/models remain governed by their own licenses and must be tracked in the eventual `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, dependency/license inventory and SBOM/provenance output.

`LICENSE-TODO.md` is therefore an intentional owner blocker for public release, not permission for the coding agent to guess. `scripts/release_gate.py` must continue to fail until a real `LICENSE` and non-placeholder third-party notices are present.
